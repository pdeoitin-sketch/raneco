            WHAT THIS IS

            About

            A clock, honestly made. Here is what Tempo is, where its numbers come from, and where it stops.

            WHAT TEMPO IS

            One dashboard, focused when it matters

            Tempo keeps your time and sky tools in one fluent scroll: the clock you live on, alarms, timer, stopwatch, world clocks, time standards, calendar, old clock, arithmetic, weather and forecast. Settings opens separately from the side, only when you ask. There is no account, backend or key to manage — it all runs in your browser.

            WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM

            Public data, named sources

            Time zones come from the IANA time zone database, vendored into the page so it works offline. Weather and forecasts are measured by Open-Meteo at a point, not a country. Your device's GPS fix, when you ask for it, is named by BigDataCloud's free gazetteer. Sun time is computed from longitude and the equation of time; the moon behind the old clock from the mean synodic cycle; everything else — timers, alarms, the calculator — is plain arithmetic on your device's clock.

            HONEST LIMITS

            What a web page cannot do

            A web page cannot wake a sleeping phone: alarms and timers ring while a Tempo tab is open, and background tabs are throttled — a 90-second grace window catches the ones that wake up late. Sound may need one click first, because browsers silence pages that were never touched. Without a network there is no weather, though every clock keeps perfect time. And nothing you do here is synced anywhere: your places, alarms and remarks live in this browser's storage and nowhere else.

            REMARKS & FEEDBACK
